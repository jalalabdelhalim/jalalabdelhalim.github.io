\section{Research Profile}
Ph.D.\ candidate in Computer Science (University of Toledo, USA; expected 12/2027)
working at the intersection of cybersecurity and machine learning. My research
covers the adversarial robustness of ML-based intrusion detection in live networks,
multimodal (text, image, video) spam and threat detection, and privacy-by-design
AI systems, from low-cost edge devices that never share raw camera images to
locally hosted large language models that keep sensitive data on-premises. Five
publications with IEEE, Springer Nature, De Gruyter Brill, and SciTePress, and
extensive hands-on experience building the experimental infrastructure behind
this work. Seeking a postdoctoral position in trustworthy and secure AI, network
security, or privacy-preserving machine learning.\par\vspace{3pt}

%------------------------------------------------------------------ RESEARCH
\section{Research Experience}
\entry{Graduate Research Assistant}{08/2021 -- present}
      {University of Toledo, Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science}{Toledo, USA}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Adversarial ML for intrusion detection:} Evaluated Random Forest,
        Decision Tree, and SVM intrusion detectors trained on CICIDS-2017 against
        black-box Genetic Algorithm and Particle Swarm Optimization attacks.
        Implemented the live-network validation pipeline on an isolated Proxmox testbed,
        reconstructing adversarial flows into PCAPs and replaying them with tcpreplay.
        Showed 100\% evasion of tree-based models, and that protocol-driven attacks
        (FTP, SSH, PortScan) stayed fully functional after perturbation while
        high-volume floods lost most of their impact (ICISSP 2026).
  \item \textbf{Multimodal spam detection:} Built a real-time, user-trained tool that
        screens text, images, and video on X/Twitter, Facebook, and Reddit and alerts
        users before they engage with a post. Benchmarked BERT, DistilBERT,
        DistilRoBERTa, ResNet-50, MobileNet, and classical baselines on 22,854 tweets
        and 12,144 images; selected MobileNet for images (92.4\% accuracy) and logistic
        regression for text on the accuracy--latency trade-off (IEEE CSCE 2023;
        De Gruyter Brill 2025).
  \item \textbf{Privacy-preserving activity monitoring:} Designed a sub-\$60 Raspberry Pi
        and camera system that classifies five daily activities of seniors living alone
        and shows caregivers an anonymized 3D model on a web dashboard instead of raw
        images. Collected and labeled 40,724 images and compared six classifiers;
        EfficientNet-B0 reached 92\% accuracy and 93\% precision (AIR-RES 2025,
        Springer CCIS).
  \item \textbf{Privacy-preserving local LLM assistant:} Integrated an on-premises large
        language model into the course lab platform as a student assistant, keeping
        all student data on local infrastructure.
  \item \textbf{AI-assisted driver situational awareness:} Co-developed a traffic-light phase
        predictor that estimates seconds-to-green from dashcam video, using ResNet-18
        features from 6-frame sequences; on 3,541 frames from two Toledo intersections,
        Gaussian Naive Bayes and Random Forest exceeded 91\% accuracy with under 3\,s
        mean error. SUMO simulation of a GLOSA-based smart driver (via TraCI) raised
        average speed by 22\% and cut total waiting time by 12\% versus baseline
        traffic at a congested intersection (IEEE MOST 2026).
\end{itemize}
