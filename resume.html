Research Experience

Graduate Research Assistant Aug. 2021 – Present08/2021 – present
University of Toledo, Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
  • Adversarial ML for intrusion detection: Evaluated Random Forest, Decision Tree, and SVM intrusion detectors trained on CICIDS-2017 against black-box Genetic Algorithm and Particle Swarm Optimization attacks. Implemented the live-network validation pipeline on an isolated Proxmox testbed, reconstructing adversarial flows into PCAPs and replaying them with tcpreplay. Showed 100% evasion of tree-based models, and that protocol-driven attacks (FTP, SSH, PortScan) stayed fully functional after perturbation while high-volume floods lost most of their impact (ICISSP 2026).
  • Multimodal spam detection: Built a real-time, user-trained tool that screens text, images, and video on X/Twitter, Facebook, and Reddit and alerts users before they engage with a post. Benchmarked BERT, DistilBERT, DistilRoBERTa, ResNet-50, MobileNet, and classical baselines on 22,854 tweets and 12,144 images; selected MobileNet for images (92.4% accuracy) and logistic regression for text on the accuracy–latency trade-off (IEEE CSCE 2023; De Gruyter Brill 2025).
  • Privacy-preserving activity monitoring: Designed a sub-$60 Raspberry Pi and camera system that classifies five daily activities of seniors living alone and shows caregivers an anonymized 3D model on a web dashboard instead of raw images. Collected and labeled 40,724 images and compared six classifiers; EfficientNet-B0 reached 92% accuracy and 93% precision (AIR-RES 2025, Springer CCIS).
  • Privacy-preserving local LLM assistant: Integrated an on-premises large language model into the course lab platform as a student assistant, keeping all student data on local infrastructure.
  • AI-assisted driver situational awareness: Co-developed a traffic-light phase predictor that estimates seconds-to-green from dashcam video, using ResNet-18 features from 6-frame sequences; on 3,541 frames from two Toledo intersections, Gaussian Naive Bayes and Random Forest exceeded 91% accuracy with under 3 s mean error. SUMO simulation of a GLOSA-based smart driver (via TraCI) raised average speed by 22% and cut total waiting time by 12% versus baseline traffic at a congested intersection (IEEE MOST 2026).
